# Case_Study_1_Bright_Coffee_Shop
Bright_Coffee_Shop_Sales_Analysis

**Overview**
- I have been appointed as a Data Analyst. Bright Coffee shop provided daily transactional data from the coffee shop. I have been requested to extract actionable insights from historical data and prepare a presentation to assist the CEO in decision-making with the following Objective:
    -	Which products generate the most revenue
    -	What time of the day the store performs best
    -	Sales trends across products and time intervals
    -	Recommendations for improving sales performance

**Insights**
  -	Sales has doubled since January by R84,8K (+104% Growth)
  -	Sales per Day grew with a MoM average of 16%
  -	Coffee and Tea contributed 66,74% of Sales
  -	The Morning traffic generated 55,56% of Sales
  -	48,8% of Sales is generated between 7am-10am
  -	Coffee, Tea and Bakery represent 85.0% of quantity sold
  -	All 3 stores grew their sales by +100% 
  -	Top 10 Sales by Product Type 
      -  % Share by Category
         - Coffee:  	         	  48.9%
         - Tea:        	              31.3%
         - Drinking Chocolate:  	  13.1%
         - Bakery:			           6.7%
-	Top 10 Sales by Product Detail
      - % Share by Category
         - Coffee:  	         	  57.3%
         - Drinking Chocolate:  	  32.9%
         - Tea:        	               9.8%

**Recommendations**
  -	Maintain and scale the morning traffic. Ensure staff capacity is adequate during the 7-10am to handle traffic and potential growth   overtime
  -	Create an event and test it in the afternoon between 2-5pm, where you create complementary combo’s specials with 10 coffee/tea’s and most selling bakery products (e.g. Dark chocolate and scones) to attract traffic and increase revenue
  -	Introduce loyalty programs where you get double points between 2-5pm.
  -	Maintain and scale the morning traffic. Ensure staff capacity is adequate during the 7-10am to handle traffic and potential growth overtime
